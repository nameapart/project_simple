Assignment 3: Epistemological Interrogation

Students will construct a critical exegesis that interrogates the socio-spatial dialectics inherent within contemporary urban architectural modalities. Utilizing a robust, multifaceted theoretical framework grounded in post-structuralist hegemony, your prose must critically deconstruct how localized municipal infrastructures inadvertently reify socio-economic stratifications. You are expected to synthesize these paradigms to illuminate the intersectional subversion of institutionalized power dynamics as they manifest macroscopically within the localized built environment.
