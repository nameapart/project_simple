Using a picture, graphically document stratification (local, national, or global) and write 130 minimum length commentary to accompany picture(s).
You can use a personal picture or one downloaded from internet. For the former, you can use a picture of yourself, family, friend(s), neighborhood, or people/places/things in your community. For the latter, you can download a picture related to stratification in general or a particular stratification topic. You can refer to the course syllabus to get some topic ideas.
Minimum number of pictures: one (1) (you may use more if you want).
Write a 130 word paragraph about the meaning of the picture(s) you selected. You can include commentary about why you selected this particular image and/or how it is relevant in today's society.
You can use either a Word document or Powerpoint to construct your assignment. Attached are examples. (Note: These were not submitted for SOC 1002 but for other classes.)
