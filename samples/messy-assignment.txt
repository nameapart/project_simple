HIST 2110 - Blog Post #3 (see also the schedule on Canvas)

For this unit we are continuing our discussion of primary source analysis as we
discussed in class. You will be writing a "blog post" (this is not a formal essay
but it should still be polished, I will be looking for college-level writing) in
which you engage with ONE of the primary sources from the Chapter 7 reading packet
- note that the packet has nine sources but three of them we already covered in
the week 4 discussion so those are off limits. Length should be appropriate to the
task, most students find 800-1000 words works, but I am more interested in depth
than length so do not pad.

You should situate your source in its historical moment. What was happening? Who
wrote it and why does that matter? Remember our conversation about audience. Do not
simply summarize the source, I can read it myself. I want your analysis.

You will need to bring in secondary scholarship. Two sources minimum from the
library databases, not Google. Chicago style footnotes please, there is a guide
linked in the Resources module, and note that the blog format still requires proper
citation even though it is informal in tone.

Posts are due Sunday night. Late posts will be marked down. You also need to respond
substantively to two of your classmates' posts by the following Wednesday - a
sentence or two is not substantive.

Grading: I use the standard rubric posted in Module 1 (analysis 40%, use of evidence
30%, writing 20%, engagement 10%).
