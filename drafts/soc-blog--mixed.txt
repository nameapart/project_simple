Illustrated Social Problem

For my blog entry I chose a photograph taken outside the Walmart on 10th Street, showing a line of cars parked overnight in the lot. Several of them have blankets taped over the windows and belongings stacked in the back seats. The specific social problem I am illustrating is housing insecurity, specifically the growing number of people who are employed but cannot afford stable housing and end up living in their vehicles.

This is a social problem and not a personal issue. A lot of people are dealing with this right now, not just one or two, and it would be unfair to say that all of those individuals simply made bad choices. When something is happening to this many people at once it stops being about any one person's decisions. The photograph shows at least six cars, and that is only one parking lot in one town on one night, so the real number across the country has to be enormous. It is clearly bigger than the individuals involved.

What makes this especially difficult is how hard it is to get out of once you are in it. If you are sleeping in your car it becomes harder to stay clean and rested, which makes it harder to hold down the job that is supposed to get you back into housing in the first place. People in this situation often need someone to give them a chance, and I think if more of us understood what was actually happening in those parking lots there would be a lot more sympathy for what they are going through.
