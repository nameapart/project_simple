SOC 101: Introduction to Sociology - Course Syllabus

Instructor: Dr. R. Alvarez
Office: Hartley 218. Office hours Tuesday and Thursday 1:00-3:00 PM, or by appointment.

Course Description
This course introduces students to the sociological perspective and the systematic
study of human social behavior. We will examine culture, socialization, social
structure, deviance, stratification, and social institutions.

Required Text
Macionis, John J. Sociology, 17th edition. Available at the campus bookstore and on
reserve in the library.

Grading

Three exams (15% each)                 45%
Research paper                         25%
Final reflection                       10%

Grades are posted in D2L within one week of the due date. The grading scale is
standard: A 90-100, B 80-89, C 70-79, D 60-69, F below 60.

Course Policies
Attendance is expected. More than three unexcused absences will lower your
participation grade by one letter.

Late work is accepted up to 72 hours past the deadline with a 30% penalty. After 72
hours, work is not accepted without documentation of an emergency.

Academic integrity violations are reported to the Dean of Students. Please review the
college policy on plagiarism.

All written work should be submitted through D2L in .docx or .pdf format.
