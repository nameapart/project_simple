SOC 2016 - Sociology of Gender
Week 1 - Participation

In order to earn Participation Points for this week, each student will be required to submit a brief introduction to the rest of the class that will include addressing the following talking points:

Name?  What do you prefer us to call you?
Where did you grow up?
Family information?
Why are you taking this class?
What are you excited to learn about regarding this subject? 
What makes you nervous/uncomfortable about this subject/class?

Please include a picture if you have one available that you can cut & paste into your response.

Each student will post their information (worth 5 points), and then will need to respond to a minimum of 2 other classmates with a quick "hello" and something that you may or may not have in common with them based upon their responses (worth 5 points each). In order to receive the full points for your responses and to make the responses meaningful, you should aim for approximately 100+ words each.

Original posts should be submitted no later than Wednesday, so your classmates have an opportunity to review your response and respond to your post if they are able to.
