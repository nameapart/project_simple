After you complete the reading and study the chapter notes, demonstrate what you learned in the discussion, "Your Perception of Social Problems":

1) Explain how social problems (i.e., homelessness, substance abuse, property crime, etc.) reflect structural inequality, a condition that reinforces unequal status as certain categories of people are subjected to systemic inequitable opportunities via the normative operations of social institutions (i.e., work & the economy, education, criminal justice system, healthcare, etc.).  For help, refer to Chapter #1 Notes and Understanding Social Problems.

1a) Using your sociological imagination, which is the ability to make the connection between individuals' personal experiences or circumstances and the 'larger' social structures of society, explain how self-agency and individuals' 'choices' are influenced by specific structural factors/social institutions, and give an example.

2) What do you consider the most pressing social problem(s) in the U.S. or globally?  Why?

3) How have you been personally impacted by or observed a social problem(s)?
